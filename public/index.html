About Shalz

Words that feel like home

Shalini — known by heart as Shalz — is a Tamil poet and podcaster who pours emotion into language with rare sincerity. Under the name ennodu inainthiru ("be with me"), she shares verses and spoken reflections that invite you to sit still with your feelings for a while.

Her work lives at the intersection of Tamil literary tradition and the intimate, unscripted texture of modern storytelling. One day, she intends to bring these stories to the screen as a director.

The Dream Behind the Words

Every poem Shalz writes, every episode she records, is a step toward a larger canvas — cinema. She believes that the precision of Tamil verse and the intimacy of podcasting are the best training ground for a director who wants to make audiences feel, not just watch.

The name ennodu inainthiru is not just a page — it's a promise: to stay with you through every medium she creates in.
